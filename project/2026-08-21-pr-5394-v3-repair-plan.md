## Decisions

Accepted invitations are immutable. A duplicate `PENDING` invitation may
receive a new matriculation number; an `ACCEPTED` invitation returns the
ordinary duplicate result without changing its admission metadata.

Auto-acceptance requires exactly one distinct active participant behind the
verified eligible account records. A missing or ambiguous identity stays
pending. Creating a participation uses the model default (`false`), and an
existing participation is never updated by invitation import.

Assessment invitation imports are bounded at 200 rows before processing. The
Manage CSV flow rejects files above 1 MiB and parsed inputs above 200 rows.
The Manage history uses an additive, stable paginated GraphQL field with a
maximum page size of 50 and keeps the old field and persisted operation intact
for rolling compatibility. Retiring the legacy unbounded field is a separate
follow-up after deployment evidence shows that old clients have drained.
