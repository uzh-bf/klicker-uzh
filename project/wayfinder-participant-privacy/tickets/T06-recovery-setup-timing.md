# T06 — Decide recovery setup timing

Label: `wayfinder:grilling`
Parent: [MAP.md](../MAP.md)
Status: open
Assignee:
Blocked by: T02

## Question

Open decision from the plan's table. Owner: Product. The plan's recommendation is "defer
recovery setup during running live activities; require it on next non-live login or after
grace period".

Once a participant's email is no longer retained, recovery depends on whatever they set
up in advance — so *when* we force that setup determines how many people get locked out.
Too eager and it interrupts a live quiz; too lax and the cutover strands people.

Rule on: when recovery setup is prompted, when it becomes mandatory, what the grace
period is, and what happens to a participant who reaches the cutover without one.

Bring the composition numbers from
[Measure participant login-method composition](T02-login-method-composition.md) —
specifically how many participants would have no usable recovery route — and put a
recommendation on the table before asking Product to rule.

The plan also wants signup funnel drop-off data. Judge whether that is genuinely needed
for this decision or whether composition settles it; if it is needed, that becomes its
own ticket rather than blocking this one indefinitely.
