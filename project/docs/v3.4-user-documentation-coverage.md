## Important boundaries before publication

- AI beta enrollment does not grant the separate AI account entitlement. The
  lecturer AI entry points require both; enrollment may be unavailable while
  registration is closed.
- Chatbot creation is self-service draft authoring, not immediate publication.
- The lecturer assistant saves SC, MC and free-text proposals. The separate
  generation workflow supports SC, MC, KPRIM and flashcards.
- Only SC/MC/KPRIM generation offers source/page scopes, difficulty, Bloom levels
  and design/plan approval gates. Flashcards skip those gates and have their own
  retry/incomplete-output recovery. Both paths require review before saving.
- No student workflow for saving personal generated flashcards was found in the
  checked branches. Do not advertise that workflow in tutorials without an
  implementation and a verified student entry point.
- Quizzer depends on the chatbot's configured course-material capability.
- The `v3-ai` branch already contains an assistant tutorial at
  `tutorials/ai_assistant.mdx`; this update uses the same route. Reconcile its
  content when combining branches. Its separate `course_ai_tutor.mdx` still
  describes team-only setup; update or consolidate that page when promoting it.
- Preserve the original PR's private-preview/deployment decisions for batch
  sharing, regular live-quiz reset and response counters. This pass does not
  change application flags or prove deployment.
- Assessment functionality (identity, invitations, results, corrections,
  reports and exports) is outside the agreed announcement and the original
  public-docs scope. This pass does not introduce assessment tutorials.
- Privacy, research policy and provider commitments need their own approved
  wording; no binding policy text was changed.
