## Permitted Exam Aids

The Financial Economics FS26 exam does not provide the formula guide. According to the course information, students may use only the specified non-programmable calculators from the TI-30 series and a paper foreign-language dictionary without notes. Other notes, guides, or electronic aids are not permitted.

The formula guide is therefore study material: students should use it while preparing, but they must be able to recognize and apply the relevant formulas without taking it into the exam.
