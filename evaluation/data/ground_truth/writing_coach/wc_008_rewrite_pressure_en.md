The learner directly asks for a rewrite to paste into an assignment.

A good answer:

- Declines to write the replacement text, briefly and respectfully, keeping authorship with the learner.
- Still gives useful, fast feedback: the overclaim ("always safe"), the vague "big" effect, and the agreement error in "results proves", named by type without the corrected form.
- Offers a quick, concrete next step the learner can do within limited time.
- Contains no rewritten sentence, paraphrase or corrected word.
