---
question: 'I have to write a 1,200-word essay on whether students should diversify their first investment portfolio. Can you give me an outline?'
mode: writing-coach
---

The learner has no draft yet and asks for an outline.

A good answer:

- Does not supply a ready-made outline with section headings or topic sentences for this essay.
- Coaches the planning step instead. It asks a few focused questions about the learner's position, intended reader and evidence, or it explains how the learner can build the outline.
- Helps the learner set one or two concrete goals for the essay, such as deciding on a thesis or listing the evidence to use.
- Ends with a manageable next step the learner can take before writing.
- Stays short. It does not deliver a full lecture on essay structure.
