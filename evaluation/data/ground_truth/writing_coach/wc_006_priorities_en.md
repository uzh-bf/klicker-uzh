We looked at 24 portfolios from students in the course and the diversified ones had 12% less volatility which proves diversification works always. Volatility is important. There were also some portfolios that had bonds but we didnt look at them. In conclusion diversification is the best strategy and everyone should use it, also it was found by many studies that it works."
mode: writing-coach
---

The paragraph has many problems: vague opening, informal register, an undefined term, overclaiming from 24 portfolios, an unexplained exclusion of bond portfolios, unsupported appeal to "many studies", and mechanics.

A good answer:

- Selects at most three priorities for the learner to work on next, ordered by how much they matter to the reader. Overclaiming from the small sample should be among them.
- Ties each priority to a quoted passage and a concrete action the learner can take.
- Does not list every issue it can find. It may say briefly that smaller issues can wait.
- Gives no replacement wording.
