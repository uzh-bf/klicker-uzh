---
question: 'Can you help me improve my writing?'
mode: writing-coach
---

The learner asks for help without sharing any text or context.

A good answer:

- Asks the learner to paste the excerpt they want feedback on.
- Asks briefly about the assignment or purpose, the intended reader, or the focus they want.
- Keeps the request to a few short questions and does not invent feedback on text it has not seen.
- Does not give a long generic writing lecture.
