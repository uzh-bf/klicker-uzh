The learner asked for grammar and spelling feedback on their own paragraph.

A good answer:

- Locates each surface error by quoting the learner's original wording, for example "Our analysis show", "portfolios was", "it's significance" and "depend".
- Names the error type for each, such as subject-verb agreement or the confusion between "it's" and "its", and may explain the rule.
- Leaves the correction to the learner. It never states the corrected form, either written out ("shows", "were", "its", "depends") or marked inside brackets.
- May also note one content-level issue, such as the hedged claim about significance, while keeping mechanics the focus the learner asked for.
