## How do I get started as a lecturer?

Sign in to [KlickerUZH Manage](https://manage.klicker.uzh.ch), then read the [core concepts](/getting_started/core_concepts/). The [Live Quiz tutorial](/tutorials/live_quiz/) walks you through preparing questions and using them in class. For work between classes, explore [Practice Quizzes](/tutorials/practice_quiz/) and [Microlearning](/tutorials/microlearning/).

## Do students need an account?

Students can join a Live Quiz anonymously using its link or QR code. Accounts are needed for features such as course participation and progress across activities. See the [student account guide](/student_tutorials/student_accounts/) for the available participation options.

## Is KlickerUZH free to use?

The core features of the public instance are free to use. Some advanced features require Catalyst access or beta participation. The [Catalyst page](/catalyst/) explains eligibility, sponsorship and how to request access.

## Where can I report a problem or suggest an improvement?

Use the [feedback platform](https://klicker-uzh.feedback.df-app.ch/) for ideas, experiences and problems. Please do not include personal or course data. For teaching questions and discussion, visit the [community](https://community.klicker.uzh.ch/). Support is offered on a best-effort basis.

## What is coming in v3.4?

The [roadmap](/development/) distinguishes available capabilities, the upcoming v3.4 preview and planned work. Preview features may require additional access or approval; a roadmap entry is not a promise of availability for every account.
