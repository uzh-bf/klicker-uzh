---
title: Escape Room
---

Some Practice Quizzes and Microlearnings are configured as an **Escape Room** by your lecturer. In this mode, the activity becomes a timed challenge: you have exactly **one attempt** to clear all stages before the countdown runs out. This section describes the **student view** of Escape Rooms; lecturers can find the corresponding setup instructions in the [lecturer tutorial](/tutorials/escape_room).

## Starting your attempt

When you open an Escape Room, you first see a start screen with the introduction from your lecturer, the number of stages, the time limit, and the hint penalty. Your attempt — and the countdown — only begins once you press **Start Attempt**, so take your time reading the introduction first.

:::warning
You only have **one attempt**. Once you start, the timer keeps running even if you close the app or lose your connection. Make sure you have enough uninterrupted time before starting. If something goes wrong, contact your lecturer, who can reset your attempt.
:::

## Playing through the stages

While your attempt is running, a bar at the top of the screen shows the remaining time and how many stages you have already cleared.

- The stages must be solved **in order**. The next stage only unlocks after you have answered the current one correctly.
- A **wrong answer** does not end your attempt: you stay on the same stage and can try again. However, each wrong answer triggers a short lockout of a few seconds before your next submission is accepted — guessing rapidly does not pay off.
- Some questions offer a **hint**. Revealing a hint deducts the penalty shown on the button from your remaining time, so use hints deliberately.
- For a **QR Scan** question, allow camera access and scan the printed station code. If scanning is unavailable, enter the code shown below the QR image manually.

## Finishing

If you clear the final stage in time, you have escaped! The completion screen shows your escape time, the number of hints you used, and the total time penalty. If the timer runs out before you finish, the attempt expires and shows how many stages you managed to clear. In both cases the result is final — only your lecturer can reset your attempt for another try.
