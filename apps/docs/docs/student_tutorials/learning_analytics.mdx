:::info
Learning Analytics is in **beta** and is available on request for users at UZH. You will only see the choice described here if your lecturer has enabled it for your course.
:::

## What you are being asked

If Learning Analytics is enabled in one of your courses, KlickerUZH asks you once whether your activity in that course may be used for it. You can participate, decline, or postpone the decision.

Learning Analytics helps your lecturers see where the group as a whole is struggling — which questions cause the most errors, how far people have got through the material, how engagement develops over the semester — so that they can adjust the course and the feedback you get.

## What happens if you participate

If you choose to participate, KlickerUZH uses:

- your interaction activity and learning progress
- your response performance and scores
- your feedback ratings (the upvotes and downvotes you give)

Only activity **from the moment you choose to participate** is used. Nothing you did before that is included, and if you later stop participating and start again, the period in between is never added retroactively.

### What your lecturers do not see

Your lecturers never see:

- your name, your email address, or your participant identifier
- your free-text responses
- your free-text feedback
- whether you chose to participate or not

Two additional rules protect you:

**Nothing is shown for fewer than five students.** Every view your lecturers get is only displayed if at least five participating students contribute to it. If fewer contribute, they see a notice instead of the data.

**Labels are shuffled for every report.** Where your lecturers see a per-student table, it uses labels such as _Student 1_ and _Student 2_. These are assigned afresh every single time a report is generated, so _Student 4_ today and _Student 4_ next week are not the same person, and the labels cannot be traced back to your account.

Your lecturers are also explicitly instructed not to try to identify individual students, and not to combine Learning Analytics results with other information in order to do so.

If you want to see exactly what this looks like from the other side, the [lecturer documentation for Learning Analytics](/tutorials/learning_analytics) shows the dashboards your lecturers work with.

## What happens if you decline

Nothing about your normal course participation changes. You keep full access to the course, all activities, the chatbot, the leaderboard, points, and XP. The only effect is that your data is not used for new Learning Analytics calculations, and any Learning Analytics results that relate to you personally are removed immediately.

Declining has no effect on your grades and is not visible to your lecturers.

## Deciding later, and changing your mind

You can postpone the decision. Until you decide, you are **not** included — the default is exclusion, not participation.

You can also change your choice at any time while Learning Analytics is enabled in the course. Open the course in the KlickerUZH app and use the Learning Analytics notice at the top of the course page.

If a lecturer turns Learning Analytics off and later turns it on again, your saved choice applies again, so you do not have to decide from scratch.

## This is not research consent

Choosing to participate in Learning Analytics is **not** consent to take part in a research project. It only covers the course dashboards described above. If you are ever asked to participate in research, that is a separate decision with its own consent process.

For the full legal picture, see the [privacy policy](/privacy_policy) and the [terms of service](/terms_of_service).
