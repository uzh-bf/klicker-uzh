Learning Analytics is in **beta** and is available on request for users at UZH. You will only see the choice described here if your lecturer has enabled it for your course.
:::

## What you are being asked

If Learning Analytics is enabled in one of your courses, KlickerUZH asks whether your activity in that course may be used for it. You can participate, decline, or postpone the decision. If you postpone, the notice remains until you make a current choice. A material change to the disclosure may require you to make a new choice before your activity becomes eligible again.

Learning Analytics helps your lecturers see where the group as a whole is struggling — which questions cause the most errors, how far people have got through the material, how engagement develops over the semester — so that they can adjust the course and the feedback you get.

## What happens if you participate

If you choose to participate, KlickerUZH uses:

- your interaction activity and learning progress
- your response performance and scores
- your feedback ratings (the upvotes and downvotes you give)

Only activity **from the moment you choose to participate** is used. Nothing you did before that is included, and if you later stop participating and start again, the period in between is never added retroactively.

### What your lecturers do not see in Learning Analytics

Within Learning Analytics, your lecturers never see:

- your name, your email address, or your participant identifier
- your free-text responses
- your free-text feedback
- exact timestamps for your individual activity
- item-level response sequences
- rare attributes or other distinctive dimensions
- stable pseudonyms or links across reports
- whether you chose to participate or not

Two additional rules protect you:

**Five-student privacy rule.** Learning Analytics uses a minimum sample size of five. A report may show a suppression notice when its overall result is below that threshold. Depending on the report and filters, lower-sample chart points, activities, or feedback breakdowns may instead be omitted without a separate notice. An omitted detail should not be read as zero activity.

**Labels are shuffled for every report.** Where your lecturers see a per-student table, it uses report-local labels such as _Student 1_ and _Student 2_. These are assigned afresh every time a report is generated, so _Student 4_ today is not intended to be linked to _Student 4_ next week or back to your account. The output is de-identified with safeguards against re-identification, not guaranteed anonymous.

Your lecturers are also explicitly instructed not to try to identify individual students, and not to combine Learning Analytics results with other information in order to do so.

These limits apply to Learning Analytics. Normal course activities and evaluation have their own access rules.

If you want to see exactly what this looks like from the other side, the [lecturer documentation for Learning Analytics](/tutorials/learning_analytics) shows the dashboards your lecturers work with.

## What happens if you decline

Nothing about your normal course participation changes. You keep access to the course, all activities, the leaderboard, points, and XP. Declining Learning Analytics does not grant or remove chatbot access; chat remains governed by its separate disclaimer. Your data is not used for new Learning Analytics calculations, and participant-level Learning Analytics results that relate to you personally are removed immediately. Existing group aggregates are updated on their normal calculation schedule and may not be recomputed immediately, so they may temporarily reflect activity that was eligible before you declined.

Declining has no effect on your grades. Your individual choice is not shown to your lecturers, although aggregate sample sizes or suppression states may change without revealing who chose what.

## Deciding later, and changing your mind

You can postpone the decision. Until you decide, you are **not** included — the default is exclusion, not participation.

You can also change your choice at any time while Learning Analytics is enabled in the course. Open the course in the KlickerUZH app and use the Learning Analytics notice at the top of the course page.

If a lecturer turns Learning Analytics off and later turns it on again, your saved choice applies again when the disclosure is still current, and permitted normal course data may be recomputed. A course-level disabled period is not categorically excluded. A period when you personally declined is never backfilled if you later participate again, and a material disclosure change requires a new choice.

## This is not research consent

Choosing to participate in Learning Analytics is **not** consent to take part in a research project. It only covers the course dashboards described above. If you are ever asked to participate in research, that is a separate decision with its own consent process.
