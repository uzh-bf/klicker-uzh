## How do I manage beta features?

The AI features described in these tutorials are **beta features** and are **enabled by default**. If you prefer not to use beta features, open **User Settings**, find **Beta features**, and disable **Beta enrollment**. You can enable them again using the same setting.

Wait for the save confirmation before leaving the page. If your preference was saved but the displayed settings could not be refreshed, reload the page to see its current state.

Your beta preference is stored with your account. Available features still depend on rollout and any required approval for AI usage. Changing the preference requires full account access; enabling it also requires Catalyst access. If the setting is restricted, use your account-owner or full-access login. Contact the KlickerUZH team if a setting or feature you need remains unavailable.

:::info
Beta features may change and are still being evaluated. Review generated content before using it with students. Having beta access enabled for your lecturer account does not publish a chatbot or give students access to a draft.
:::

## How do I request Catalyst access?

Where the application offers **Request Catalyst Access**, open the form, enter your **Institution** and **Intended Use**, and select **Send Request**. The team replies to your account email. Submitting a request does not immediately activate access.

Catalyst access requests and beta enrollment serve different purposes: use the request form for access to restricted functionality, and the setting to disable or re-enable the beta features offered to your account.
