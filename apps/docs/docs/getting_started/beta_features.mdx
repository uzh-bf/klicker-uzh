## How do I enable beta features?

The AI features described in these tutorials are **beta features**. To unlock the beta features available to your account, open **User Settings**, find **Beta features**, and enable **Beta enrollment**. You can opt out again using the same setting.

Wait for the confirmation before leaving the page. Access may take up to two minutes to update. If enrollment was saved but access could not be refreshed, reload the page and try again.

Beta enrollment is available when registration is open and your account is eligible. Individual features may also require additional account access or configuration. If the setting or a feature is missing, contact the KlickerUZH team; enrollment does not automatically grant every restricted feature.

:::info
Beta features may change and are still being evaluated. Review generated content before using it with students. Enabling beta access for your lecturer account does not publish a chatbot or give students access to a draft.
:::

## Which AI tutorials are available?

- [Course chatbots](/tutorials/chatbot/): create a chatbot draft, configure its learning modes, and request publication.
- [KlickerUZH Assistant](/tutorials/ai_assistant/): ask for help and prepare question drafts in the management application.
- [Knowledge bases and graphs](/tutorials/knowledge_bases/): organize materials and explore connections between their concepts.
- [AI element generation](/tutorials/ai_element_generation/): generate and review elements for your library.

## How do I request Catalyst access?

Where the application offers **Request Catalyst Access**, open the form, enter your **Institution** and **Intended Use**, and select **Send Request**. The team replies to your account email. Submitting a request does not immediately activate access.

Catalyst access requests and beta enrollment serve different purposes: use the request form for access to restricted functionality, and the setting to opt in to the beta features offered to your account.
