:::caution Availability
Learning analytics is part of a flag-controlled rollout and is off by default.
This page describes the V2 lecturer view when the rollout is enabled for your
account. It does not mean that the feature is available for every account or
course.
:::

## Who is included in learning analytics?

Each participant has one current learning-analytics choice for their entire
KlickerUZH account. This is not a course-specific choice. Individual data can
only appear after the participant has chosen **Yes** and the course has
completed a later successful recomputation. Choosing **No** excludes the
participant from individual learning analytics.

A course manager controls learning analytics separately for each course. New
courses start with learning analytics disabled. Disabling it hides the course
dashboards immediately. Enabling it does not make a previous report available:
the dashboards remain unavailable until a later recomputation succeeds.

## What does the V2 report show?

V2 provides a deliberately limited view of course activity and progress:

- Weekly activity uses ordinal labels such as `Period 1`. It does not expose
  calendar dates or weekdays.
- Activity summaries use ordinal labels such as `Activity 1` and identify only
  the activity type. Reported percentages use coarse ten-point steps.
- The student report uses freshly randomized, report-local labels such as
  `Student 1`. These labels can change whenever a report or export is created
  and cannot be used to follow one participant between reports.

KlickerUZH suppresses every V2 report or cell that has fewer than five eligible
participants. Counts from one through four are never shown. Ordinal labels are
assigned after suppression, so their sequence does not reveal omitted periods
or activities. A released cell also remains hidden when comparison with its
parent total would reveal a suppressed group of one through four.

Student rows are released only when at least five candidates have the same
coarse combination of completed activities and mean completion. Smaller groups
and their size are not shown. The effective participant count describes the
released report, not the course roster.

V2 has no quiz or item breakdown, daily or weekday view, activity-detail view,
or course comparison. It does not disclose names, email addresses, account or
participant identifiers, free-text responses, exact timestamps, stable
activity identifiers, item sequences, or rare attributes.

The report is de-identified, but it is not guaranteed to be anonymous. You must
therefore continue to treat it as protected course data.

## What can I export?

The JSON and CSV exports use the same fixed fields as the V2 student report:

- schema version and effective participant count;
- the temporary `Student N` label;
- completed activity count; and
- mean completion percentage in a ten-point step.

Use learning-analytics reports and exports only for teaching. Store and share
exports securely. Do not try to identify participants or link the output with
other data sources. Research use requires separate participant consent and is
not covered by the learning-analytics choice or this lecturer feature.
