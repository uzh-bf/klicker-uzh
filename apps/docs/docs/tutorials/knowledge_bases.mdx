## What is a knowledge base?

A knowledge base is a collection of your teaching materials that KlickerUZH prepares for its AI features. A [course chatbot](/tutorials/chatbot/) connected to a knowledge base answers from these materials and cites them, and [AI element generation](/tutorials/question_generation/) creates question drafts from them.

A knowledge base belongs to your account and can be reused: you can connect the same knowledge base to several chatbots.

Knowledge bases are available to Catalyst lecturers with beta features enabled and AI features approved for their account (see [Who can create a chatbot?](/tutorials/chatbot/#who-can-create-a-chatbot)).

## How do I create a knowledge base?

Open **Resources** → **Knowledge Bases** and choose **Create knowledge base**. Give it a name and, optionally, a description. You can also create a knowledge base directly from the **Materials** section of the chatbot setup.

In the knowledge base settings, choose the **subject area** and the **language** of your materials. They are used whenever KlickerUZH prepares the materials for question generation.

## How do I add materials?

Choose **Add resource** and select the type:

- **Document:** Upload PDF, TXT or Markdown files of up to 25 MB each. You can drop several files at once.
- **Website:** Register the URL of a public web page.
- **Video:** Direct video and lecture-recording import is not available yet. If your course relies on recordings, contact the KlickerUZH team.

For each resource, you can set a **material category** (course content or administrative). After you add a resource, it is processed in the background. The resource list shows whether each resource is processing, ready or failed, and you can replace a file with a newer version.

The knowledge base overview shows how many resources it contains, how much of your storage quota is used and which chatbots are connected.

:::info
Only add materials you are allowed to share with your students. A chatbot can quote and link the materials of its knowledge base in its answers.
:::

## How is a knowledge base used?

- **Chatbots:** Connect the knowledge base in the **Materials** section of a chatbot. For a published chatbot, changes to its materials apply to students immediately.
- **Question generation:** KlickerUZH prepares the materials for question generation automatically. The knowledge base shows when it is ready for question generation.
- **Knowledge graph:** From the prepared materials, KlickerUZH can build a knowledge graph of the concepts and their relationships. In a chatbot, you can show this graph to students as a concept map and use it to improve the search in the materials.

## How do I remove a knowledge base?

You can delete individual resources or the whole knowledge base. Deleted materials are removed in the background and are no longer used by connected chatbots.
