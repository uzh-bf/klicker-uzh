## What is AI element generation?

AI element generation creates drafts of KlickerUZH elements from the materials in one of your [knowledge bases](/tutorials/knowledge_bases/). It supports **single choice**, **multiple choice**, **KPRIM** and **flashcards**. Every generated element goes through your review before it is saved to your question pool, so you stay responsible for its correctness, wording and suitability.

:::info
AI element generation is being rolled out gradually. If **Generate elements** is not shown in your account, or the page says that generation is not yet available for your account, it has not been activated for you yet. It additionally requires AI features to be approved for your account.
:::

## How do I start a generation?

Open **Generate elements** from the question pool. Only knowledge bases that are ready for question generation can be selected.

1. **Knowledge source:** Select a knowledge base. You can use all of its sources or limit the generation to selected sources and page ranges.
2. **Element type:** Choose single choice, multiple choice, KPRIM or flashcard.
3. **Generation settings:** Set the number of elements (1 to 20), the language (German or English), the difficulty and the levels of Bloom's taxonomy to cover (remember, understand, apply, analyze, evaluate).
4. **Focus and objectives (optional):** Narrow the batch to a focus topic and add learning objectives. Write observable, specific objectives, for example "Explain why diversification reduces unsystematic risk".

Choose **Generate elements** to start. Generation runs in the background, so you can continue working and return to it later.

## What do I review?

Depending on the generation, you are asked to confirm intermediate steps before generation continues:

- **Generation design:** how the requested elements are distributed across the topics and objectives of your materials.
- **Element plan:** the planned prompts, cognitive levels, difficulty and source coverage per element.

Warnings, for example when several elements rely on the same evidence, are shown at these steps and have to be acknowledged before you continue. You can also reject the generation.

## How do I keep the generated elements?

When generation is complete, review the generated elements in the list. For each element, you see its type, Bloom level, difficulty and the sources it is based on, including the page. Elements flagged for quality review are marked as needing attention.

- **Keep element** saves the element, including your edits, to your question pool.
- **Discard** removes it from the selection. You can restore it later.
- **Request revision** asks for a reworked version of a single element, optionally with a note on what should change.

If some elements could not be generated, for example because the materials do not cover a requested objective, the generation is marked as partially ready. You can keep the elements that were generated; the overview names the topics your materials do cover.

## What should I keep in mind?

- Review every element before using it in an activity. Generated content can be wrong, imprecise or too easy or too hard for your course.
- Generation uses only the materials in the selected knowledge base. Better structured materials lead to better elements.
- For drafting individual questions in a conversation, you can also use the [KlickerUZH Assistant](/tutorials/ai_assistant/).
