## What is the KlickerUZH Assistant?

![KlickerUZH Assistant in the lecturer interface](/img/ai_assistant/manage_assistant_drawer.png)

The KlickerUZH Assistant is an AI chat companion built into the lecturer interface. You open it with the **Assistant** button in the bottom right corner of any page and it appears as a chat panel next to your work, so you do not have to leave the page you are on. Using the icon in the panel header, you can also open the assistant in a separate browser tab.

The assistant knows which part of KlickerUZH you are currently working in (for example your question pool, a course, an activity wizard, or an evaluation) and offers matching starting points for a conversation. This context is only a hint for the conversation — it never grants the assistant access to anything you could not access yourself.

:::info
The assistant is currently being rolled out gradually. If you do not see the **Assistant** button in your account yet, the feature has not been activated for you.
:::

## What can the assistant help me with?

![Assistant answering a question about element types](/img/ai_assistant/manage_assistant_response.png)

The assistant works with your own KlickerUZH content and supports three kinds of tasks:

1. **Search your courses and question pool:** It can list the courses you have access to, summarize a single course with its activity counts, and search your questions by topic — including drafts. Use this to find reusable material before creating something new.
2. **Draft new questions:** It can draft single choice (SC), multiple choice (MC), and free text questions — for choice questions including answer options and sample solutions. Drafts only become real content after you confirm them (see the next section).
3. **Improve answer feedback:** It can suggest clearer, more specific feedback for the answer options of a question you are working on.

Beyond that, the assistant behaves like a discussion partner for teaching questions: you can ask it to brainstorm topics, balance the difficulty of a planned quiz, or think through results you describe to it.

It is important to know what the assistant **cannot** do:

- It cannot publish, edit, delete, duplicate, or share anything. Existing questions and activities are never modified by the assistant.
- It cannot see student responses, results, evaluations, leaderboards, or any participant data. If you want it to help you interpret results, you have to describe them yourself.
- It only sees content that your own account may access in KlickerUZH. It cannot look into courses or questions of other lecturers unless these were shared with you.
- If you are signed in through a [delegated access](/tutorials/delegated_access/) login with read-only rights, the drafting functionality is unavailable and the assistant can only search and summarize.

## How are questions created — the proposal principle

The assistant never writes into your question pool on its own. Whenever you ask it to create or save a question, it responds with a **proposal card** labelled "Confirmation required" instead of changing anything:

1. The card shows a **preview** of the drafted question with its answer options and feedback, so you can check the content before anything is stored. The underlying raw data can be expanded as well.
2. Choosing **Create draft** stores the question in your question pool with the status **Draft**, exactly as if you had created it through the [element management](/tutorials/element_management/) dialog. You are notified once the draft exists and it appears in your library.
3. Choosing **Dismiss** discards the proposal. Nothing is stored.

After you confirm a proposal, its card switches to **Draft created** and no longer offers confirmation. Since new questions always arrive as drafts, they are never visible to students before you review them, complete them, and use them in an activity.

## Can I attach images to my messages?

Yes. You can attach up to **two images per message** in the assistant, for example a photograph of a slide, a diagram, or a screenshot that a question should be based on. Supported formats are JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, and HEIC, with a maximum of 5 MB per image after conversion. If an attachment is rejected, the assistant tells you why directly above the message field.

## What should I keep in mind when using the assistant?

- **Always review AI output before it reaches students.** The assistant can be wrong, incomplete, or subtly imprecise — especially with sample solutions, numbers, and domain-specific wording. Treat every draft as a starting point that you verify and edit before publishing it in an activity.
- **Keep personal data out of the conversation.** Do not paste student names, email addresses, matriculation numbers, or other personal information into the chat.
- **Errors are safe.** If a response fails, the assistant shows a short "Something went wrong. Please try again." note. Your conversation is preserved, no content is changed, and you can simply send the message again.
