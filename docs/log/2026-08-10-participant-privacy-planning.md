# Participant privacy planning

## 2026-08-10

- **Update**: [auth-model](../auth-model.md) documents the participant privacy
  migration constraints: current email-dependent authentication and linking,
  the verified LTI 1.3 email fallback, course-aware account merging, and the
  lack of a reliable general participant inbox.
- **Creation**: [agents/issue-tracker](../agents/issue-tracker.md),
  [agents/triage-labels](../agents/triage-labels.md), and
  [agents/domain](../agents/domain.md) record the repository configuration that
  engineering skills read. GitHub Issues is the agent-facing tracker while
  ClickUp remains the product source of truth.
